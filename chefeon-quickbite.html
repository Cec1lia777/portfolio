TITLEChefeon QuickBite
宿舍半自动烹饪系统
TYPEProduct Strategy
DATE2025.08
AWARDChefeon (Shanghai Changshan), supervisor Dr. Steve Hua (Caltech PhD)
ROLEProduct strategy & UX research
TOOLSUser Research · Service System · Business Plan

A semi-automatic dorm cooking system based on the Chefeon cooking robot — integrated product design and service system planning. Many international students find it hard to access affordable, fresh and authentic Chinese meals on campus: expensive and poor access, little time to cook, and a craving for authentic flavour. QuickBite provides a convenient, affordable, authentic Chinese food option near student housing, with a numbered ingredient-pack system and a self-cleaning machine workflow.
