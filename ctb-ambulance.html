TITLEAdvanced Ambulance Avoidance System
救护车智能让行系统
TYPEResearch + Hardware
DATE2023.09 — 2024.06
AWARD3rd Place, CTB Global Finals at Harvard; paper on Young Scholars Academic
ROLETeam lead, prototyping, media campaign
TOOLSSTM32 · Bluetooth · 433MHz · LCD · Mobile App

Based on the 45-degree angled parking, this project aims to improve the efficiency of citizens giving way to ambulances, reduce treatment delays caused by congestion, and save more lives by innovating hardware equipment and raising citizens’ awareness of giving way. A give-way reminder system was designed and simulated to break down the communication barrier between ambulances and private cars; we actively promoted the 45-degree angled parking through public service advertisements on new-media platforms, reaching 6,700+ views.
