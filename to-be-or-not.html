To Be or Not?

Interactive Installation · 2025.06 — 2025.09

2025.06 — 2025.09 Northwestern Bioethics (A) · Arduino · TouchDesigner · 180×205cm

A time-based interactive installation on end-of-life decisions. Visitors face patient C-1137 and steer a kinetic spine sculpture through a projection-mapped hospital corridor — choosing life speeds up the machinery of treatment; choosing death slows everything to silence. Born from my Northwestern bioethics course (grade A).
