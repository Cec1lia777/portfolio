TITLELumiReed
视障儿童眼动助读器
TYPEAssistive Product
DATE2023.10 — 2024.06
AWARDSelf-initiated, with Bethel China
ROLEProduct design, user research, testing
TOOLSEye-tracking · Product Design · TypeScript · Tobii

An assistive reading tool for visually-impaired kids based on eye-tracking technology. When I taught English to a visually-impaired child, I noticed that he sometimes struggled to find the right page or locate the word — watching him hunched over his desk, straining to see, I started to wonder if there could be a simpler, more supportive tool. The device follows the child's gaze to zoom, highlight and recolour text, validated through experiments, interviews and a Tobii eye-tracking session.
